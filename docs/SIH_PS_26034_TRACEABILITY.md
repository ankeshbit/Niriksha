# SIH 2026 Problem Statement 26034 — Final Requirement Traceability Matrix

**Project:** NiriKsha — AI-Assisted Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities Compliance Verification System  
**Problem Statement:** PS 26034 — *“Software System to check compliance of Packaged Commodities under Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 by scanning products, images and labels.”*  
**Statutory Framework:** Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (PCR 2011)  
**Database Technology:** PostgreSQL / Neon PostgreSQL Only (Strict Anti-SQLite Architecture)  
**Verification Principle:** Evidence-First & Human-in-the-Loop (AI assists, deterministic rules evaluate, evidence supports, Inspecting Officer decides)

## 2. Statutory Legal Reference Mapping (PCR 2011)

| Rule Reference | Description | NiriKsha Implementation | Verification State |
|----------------|-------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| **Rule 6(1)(a)** | Name and complete address of the manufacturer / packer / importer | Format validation checks for premise, town, state/PIN; cross-image panel checks | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(b)** | Country of origin on imported packages | Conditional applicability; verified only if foreign manufacturer or imported commodity | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(c)** | Net quantity in standard units of weight, measure, or number | SI metric unit validation (g, kg, ml, l); PDP bottom-panel placement check | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(d)** | Month and year of manufacture, packing, or import | Multi-format date parsing; future-date plausibility check | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(e)** | Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes | Statutory qualifier check ("Inclusive of all taxes" / "Incl. of all taxes") | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(f)** | Common or generic name of the commodity | Commodity name extraction and verification; PDP placement check | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 6(1)(g)** | Name, address, telephone number, and email of consumer care | Contact channel validation (phone / email / address) | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 7 & 12** | Principal Display Panel (PDP) declarations | Spatial geometry analysis; net quantity and commodity name required on front PDP | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 9 Table 1** | Minimum height of numerals and letters for net quantity declarations | Physical scale calibration check; strict anti-fabrication without physical reference | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 18(2A)** | E-commerce / digital marketplace declaration consistency | Multi-modal listing comparison module with inspector adjudication | **VERIFIED** |
| **Rule 19** | Physical quantity limitation notice | Explicit statutory notice that images only evaluate printed labels, not physical mass | **VERIFIED** |
